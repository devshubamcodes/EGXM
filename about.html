What is EGXM?

EGXM is a simple practice platform designed to help students improve their programming skills through practice questions and quizzes.

🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to make regular programming practice simple, accessible, and useful for students.

📚 What EGXM Currently Offers

  • C Programming practice questions
  • Simple quiz interface
  • Practice-based learning
  • Student-friendly design

🚀 Future Vision

EGXM can gradually expand with more subjects, larger question banks, performance tracking, and other useful learning features.
